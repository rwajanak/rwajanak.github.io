Care, simplified

💧

Water wisely

Most plants prefer a thorough soak only when the top inch of soil is dry. When in doubt, wait a day.

☀️

Find the light

Bright, indirect light keeps foliage lush. We label every plant with its ideal spot.

🌡️

Keep it cozy

Indoor plants love 65–80°F and a little humidity. Skip the cold drafts and heat vents.

🌵🌿🍃

Grown by people who love plants

Verdant started in a sunlit apartment with three pots and a long list of plants we wanted to share. Today we propagate everything in our own greenhouse, so each plant arrives healthy, rooted, and ready for its new home.

Every order is packed by hand and shipped in recyclable, plant-safe materials. If your plant doesn't arrive thriving, we'll make it right — that's our 30-day guarantee.
