Care, simplified

Water wisely

Most plants prefer a thorough soak only when the top inch of soil is dry. When in doubt, wait a day.

☀️

Find the light

Bright, indirect light keeps foliage lush. We label every plant with its ideal spot.

Right plant, right spot

Match each plant to its home — cozy and humid indoors, or the right sun and season outdoors. Ask us and we'll point you the right way.
